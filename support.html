Support

Help, and how the beta works.

Joining the beta

The beta runs through Apple's TestFlight and is limited to 50 people to begin with, so every piece of feedback can be read and answered.

  • Ask for an invite with the button on the home page. Use the email on your Apple Account.
  • If there's a spot, you'll get an email from TestFlight. Install TestFlight from the App Store, open the email on your iPhone or iPad, and tap View in TestFlight.
  • You need iOS or iPadOS 27.1 or later. The Apple Watch app installs with the iPhone app.

Before you rely on it

It is beta software, provided as is. It has run a full show without losing data, and it can still have bugs. Export a backup from Settings → Backup regularly, and read the beta terms.

Sending feedback

The most useful feedback says what you were trying to do and what happened. Two ways to send it:

  • In the app: Settings → About → Send Feedback.
  • From TestFlight: take a screenshot in the app, tap its thumbnail, then Share Beta Feedback.

Screenshots can show shot names and notes. Turn on the Privacy Lock (the eye button) before taking one, or leave the screenshot out.

Common questions

My other device is empty

Both devices need to be signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud on for the app. The first sync can take a few minutes on Wi-Fi. If nothing arrives, close the app completely and open it again.

The overtime on my timecard is wrong

Check Settings → Week & Overtime. You can set your own 1.5× and 2× lines, turn overtime off, or, on a Guild show, change how you're paid and switch off individual rules under Settings → This Show. The app flags hours; it never calculates pay, so always check against your own timecard.

I finished a show. Now what?

Wrap it from Pulse → Show Details. Its time and pipeline lock, your notes stay readable, and you can export a reel breakdown and timecard. It then lives under Past Shows.

How do I move to a new phone?

With iCloud on, sign in on the new phone and open the app. Without iCloud, export a backup from Settings → Backup and restore it on the new device.
