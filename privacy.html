Privacy policy

Your notes stay with you.

Last updated 18 November 2026

Could Be Better ("CBB", "the app") is a personal notebook for production work. It is made by Afonso Salcedo, an individual developer. This page says what the app does with your information, in plain words.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts and no server of its own.
  • What you write is stored on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud database.
  • The developer cannot see your shows, shots, notes or hours.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the app or on this website.
  • The only personal details the developer ever holds are the ones you choose to send: a beta request, an email, or TestFlight feedback.

What the app stores, and where

Shows, shots, notes, review history, logged time and settings are saved on the device you use. If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is on for the app, that data syncs between your own devices through Apple's CloudKit, in your private database. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Some preferences, such as rounding and overtime lines, sync through iCloud's key-value store in the same way.

What the developer receives

Nothing from the app itself. The developer only receives what you choose to send:

  • The beta request form. If you ask for an invite on this website, you give your name, email address, what you do, and optionally where you work, whether your timecard is under a union agreement, which devices you use, and a note. This is used only to choose and invite beta testers and to understand their feedback. It is not sold, shared or used for marketing. It is deleted when the beta ends, or sooner if you ask at the address below. The form is delivered by email or by a form-handling service acting on the developer's behalf.
  • Email. If you write to the support address, your email address and whatever you include are used to reply to you, and not for anything else.
  • TestFlight. During the beta, Apple's TestFlight shares with the developer your tester email if you were invited by email, the feedback and screenshots you choose to submit, and crash reports. Check a screenshot for production content before you send it.
  • App Store. Apple provides developers with aggregate, non-identifying figures such as download counts, and crash data if you have opted in to sharing it with developers.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Face ID or Touch ID, only to unlock the app's Privacy Lock. The app never receives your biometric data.
  • Notifications, for timer reminders and warnings about long days. They are scheduled on your device.

Your studio's rules

Could Be Better does not connect to any studio system. Whether you may keep personal notes about a production, and what you may write in them, is a matter between you and your employer. The app gives you a Privacy Lock to hide content on screen, and you decide what goes into it.

Exporting and deleting

You can export everything from Settings → Backup at any time. Deleting a show in the app removes it from your devices and your iCloud. Deleting the app removes its data from that device; to remove the iCloud copy too, use Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your iPhone or iPad.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting anything it does not collect today, that will be said plainly, here and in the app, before it happens.
